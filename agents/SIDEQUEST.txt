SIDEQUEST
Concept: A multiplayer game layered onto real life. Friends form a Party, complete real-world SideQuests, challenge each other, bet virtual credits on outcomes, discover dynamically spawned activities, and organize larger group activities called Boss Raids.
Design feeling: See sidequest.png and sidequest.fig from design folder.
Core Navigation
Design around 5 primary areas:
MAP · QUESTS · PARTY · FEED · PROFILE
The Map should feel like the "home world" and ideally be the default screen.
________________


1. MAP — The Game World
This is the primary experience.
Show:
* User's current location
* Party members currently sharing location
* Nearby SideQuest objectives
* Random/adaptively spawned quests
* Multiplayer quests
* Boss Raids
* Interesting locations/objectives
* Temporary/limited-time events
Quests should appear as game-like markers rather than ordinary map pins.
Example:
🍓 SIDEQUEST SPAWNED
300m away
Try something you've never eaten before
🪙 +30
Expires in 42 min
If multiple friends are nearby:
⚡ MULTIPLAYER QUEST AVAILABLE
3 Party members nearby
Find somewhere none of you have eaten before.
Design goal: Opening the map should create the feeling that things are happening around me right now.
________________


2. QUESTS
Central place for the player's activities.
Sections could include:
ACTIVE — Current accepted quests with timers, progress, rewards and verification requirements.
NEARBY — Quests currently spawned around the player.
CHALLENGES — Quests friends have challenged the player to complete.
MY LIST — Things the user has said they want/need to do.
Examples:
📦 Return Amazon package
🍜 Try Korean BBQ
🧗 Go rock climbing
🛒 Buy groceries
SideQuest can transform these into actual quests at appropriate moments.
________________


3. CHALLENGES
A player can challenge another Party member to complete something.
Example:
⚔️ CHALLENGE BEN
Go to the gym before 9 PM
🪙 50 at stake
📍 GPS verification
Ben receives:
⚔️ ZURI CHALLENGED YOU
Go to the gym before 9 PM.
ACCEPT · DECLINE
Declining has no penalty.
Once accepted, the player is committed.
Complete → win Coins.
Fail → lose Coins.
Accepting should feel like accepting a duel.
________________


4. BETTING (prediction market off kalshi)
Once somebody accepts a quest, Party members can bet virtual Coins 🪙 on:
COMPLETE or FAIL
Example:
🎯 WILL BEN ACTUALLY GO?
✅ COMPLETE — 🪙80
❌ FAIL — 🪙210
47 minutes remaining
PLACE BET
Winning bettors receive Coins from the losing pool.
Self-Bounties
Players can put Coins behind their own promise.
🎯 ZURI PUT A BOUNTY ON THEMSELF
Finish assignment before midnight.
Zuri's stake: 🪙100
Friends betting against: 🪙240
The emotional loop is:
"I bet you won't." → "Watch me."
Coins are virtual game currency only, not purchasable or redeemable for money.
________________


5. QUEST VERIFICATION
Keep verification extremely simple. SideQuest uses only three verification types:
📍 GPS
The player must physically reach a location.
Examples:
* Go to the gym
* Visit a specific restaurant
* Reach a viewpoint
* Go to the grocery store
When they arrive:
📍 LOCATION VERIFIED
📸 PHOTO
The player must take a photo proving they completed the objective.
Examples:
* Try a weird food
* Find something purple
* Cook dinner
* Clean your room
* Find a specific object
The app can optionally use image recognition to check whether the photo reasonably matches the quest.
📸 PHOTO VERIFIED
⏱ TIME
The quest must be completed before a deadline or within a time window.
Examples:
* Go to the gym before 9 PM
* Reach the restaurant within 30 minutes
* Finish your assignment tonight
Time is often combined with GPS or photo verification.
⏱ COMPLETED WITH 03:42 REMAINING
A quest can therefore simply specify:
Verification: 📍 GPS + ⏱ Time
or
Verification: 📸 Photo + ⏱ Time
No QR codes, NFC, manual check-ins, or complicated verification systems are needed for the initial product.
________________


6. ADAPTIVE QUEST SPAWNING
SideQuest generates activities based on context:
* User interests
* Want/Need list
* Location
* Time
* Nearby places
* Nearby Party members
* Previous quest behavior
Example:
User likes food and says they want to try more bakeries.
Walking near a bakery triggers:
🥐 SIDEQUEST SPAWNED
Find a pastry you've never eaten.
140m away · 🪙25
The Quest Spawned moment should feel exciting and unexpected.
________________


7. MULTIPLAYER PROXIMITY QUESTS
When Party members happen to be physically near one another, SideQuest can automatically spawn multiplayer activities.
⚡ PARTY MEMBERS DETECTED
Zuri, Alyssa & Ben are nearby.
MULTIPLAYER SIDEQUEST SPAWNED
Find somewhere none of you have eaten before.
🪙150 Party Reward
The app should make spontaneous proximity feel like a game event.
________________


8. PARTY
A Party is the persistent friend group.
Show:
* Avatar
* Coins
* Current quest
* Quests completed
* Quests failed
* Challenge record
* Betting record
* Streak
* Boss Raid participation
* Funny titles/achievements
Stats should primarily encourage friendly competition and trash talk.
________________


9. PARTY FEED + CHAT
The Party has a shared game/event feed.

"Find the weirdest drink under $5."

"Saturday IKEA Expedition"
Players can react, comment, trash-talk, bet, or join activities directly from the feed.
________________


10. PROFILE / PERSONALIZATION
Players tell SideQuest enough about themselves to generate good quests.
I LIKE
Food, hiking, photography, thrifting, weird stores...
I WANT TO TRY
Rock climbing, pottery, Korean BBQ...
I NEED TO DO
Groceries, laundry, homework, exercise...
QUEST STYLE
Chill ←────────→ Unhinged
SOCIAL
Solo / Friends / Either
This should feel like configuring your player character, not filling out a questionnaire.
